Project

York Hospital Secure Care Baby Unit strip out

Internal strip out in one of the most sensitive environments there is: a working hospital baby unit.

Location
York Hospital
Sector
Healthcare
Services
Soft strip

The project

We were appointed to carry out the internal strip out for the refurbishment of the Secure Care Baby Unit (SCBU) at York Hospital. The work involved removing ceilings, floor finishes, internal walls and associated fixtures and fittings, ready for the next stage of the refurbishment.

The strip out itself was straightforward. The challenge was the setting. Keeping the work safe, clean and controlled was essential, with strict precautions to protect patients, staff and hospital operations.

Careful coordination and phased working

We kept in close, constant contact with the principal contractor and the hospital throughout. Work was planned around the hospital's operations and carried out in controlled phases.

The more intrusive activities were scheduled outside normal hours where needed. Access, working areas, noise, dust and waste movements were all managed so the surrounding areas stayed safe and operational.

What we did

  • Internal strip out of the Secure Care Baby Unit
  • Removed ceilings and floor finishes
  • Removed internal walls and associated elements
  • Strict dust, noise and contamination controls
  • Phased working around hospital operations
  • Out-of-hours working where required
  • Close coordination with the principal contractor and hospital

The outcome

The strip out was completed safely and efficiently with minimal disruption to the hospital, thanks to careful planning, good communication and a tightly controlled way of working.
