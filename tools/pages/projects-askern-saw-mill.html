The project

We demolished and cleared the former Askern Saw Mill, a large industrial site made up of several buildings and extensive areas of hardstanding.

As well as asbestos within several of the buildings, a large quantity of asbestos-containing material had been stockpiled on site. All of it was carefully managed, removed and disposed of in line with the relevant regulations and environmental requirements.

Crushing and reuse on site

A large reinforced concrete pad covered much of the site. We broke it out, then processed and crushed it on site. Suitable crushed material was reused within the project, reducing the need for imported aggregate. Everything else was processed and sent for recycling or proper disposal.

What we did

  • Demolished multiple large industrial buildings
  • Removed and disposed of significant quantities of asbestos
  • Cleared stockpiled asbestos-containing materials
  • Broke out and processed extensive concrete hardstanding
  • Crushed demolition materials on site and reused suitable material
  • Recycled or responsibly disposed of remaining waste
  • Cleared the site ready for its next phase

The outcome

A complex industrial site, with hazardous materials, heavy concrete structures and large volumes of waste, cleared safely and efficiently, with as much material as possible recycled or reused.
