Dealt with before work starts

Asbestos has to be found and dealt with before a building is refurbished or demolished. We plan this into the job from the start, working with your surveyors and consultants and, where the law requires it, with licensed asbestos specialists.

Once we know what's there, the work is carefully controlled, with containment, segregation, PPE, air monitoring and waste handling put in place as needed.

How it works

  • An asbestos survey identifies what's present before work starts
  • We plan removal into the programme with your consultants
  • Licensed specialists are used where the work legally requires a licence
  • Controls protect our team, other contractors and building occupants
  • Asbestos waste is handled, transported and documented correctly

Do I need an asbestos survey?

Usually, yes. Before refurbishment or demolition, the Health and Safety Executive expects a refurbishment and demolition survey, and asbestos must be removed as far as reasonably practicable before the main works. If you haven't had one done, tell us when you get in touch and we'll point you in the right direction. See our FAQs.
