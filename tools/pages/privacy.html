What we collect and why

When you use our enquiry form or email us, we collect the details you give us: usually your name, company, phone number, email address, site postcode and information about your project. We use this to reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit and prepare a quote. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Who we share it with

Enquiry form submissions are passed to us by a form-handling service (Web3Forms), which acts on our behalf. Our email is provided by Zoho. We don't sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry doesn't lead to work, we delete your details within 12 months. If we work together, we keep project records for as long as we need to for legal, insurance and accounting purposes.

Cookies

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies, and we don't use analytics that identify you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Just email us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
