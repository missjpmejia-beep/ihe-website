How much does demolition cost?

It depends on the size and type of structure, access, whether asbestos is present, and what happens to the materials. That's why we visit most sites before quoting. Quotes are free, and we'll explain what's included so you can compare like with like.

Do I need permission to demolish a building?

Often, yes. In England, you usually need to give your local council's building control team at least six weeks' notice before demolishing a building (a Section 80 notice under the Building Act 1984). There are exemptions, for example for buildings of 50 cubic metres or less, and for internal work in a building that stays occupied.

Planning rules may also apply. Many demolitions need a prior approval application to the local planning authority first, and listed buildings or those in conservation areas may need extra consent. We can talk you through what applies to your project.

Do I need an asbestos survey before demolition?

Usually, yes. Before refurbishment or demolition, the Health and Safety Executive expects a refurbishment and demolition survey, and asbestos must be removed as far as reasonably practicable before the main work starts. If you don't have a survey yet, tell us when you get in touch.

What happens to the waste?

We run our own licensed waste transfer station. Materials are segregated on site, recycled wherever possible, and every load is documented. On suitable jobs, we crush concrete and reuse it on site.
