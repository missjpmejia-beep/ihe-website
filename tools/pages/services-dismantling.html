When knocking it down isn't the answer

Sometimes a structure has to come down carefully: next to a building that's staying, in a live site, or where parts need to be salvaged. Dismantling lets us take it apart in a controlled order rather than demolishing it in one go.

We assess every job first to identify the risks and choose the safest method. Then we use the right mix of equipment, skilled operatives and controlled techniques to do it.

What we dismantle

  • Individual structural elements and building components
  • Plant, equipment and installations
  • Larger structures, in stages
  • Work next to buildings, roads and public areas
  • Work in live or sensitive environments

Protecting what stays

Our priority is protecting existing structures, neighbouring areas, people on site and the public. That means careful planning, clear exclusion zones and a method that keeps the work under control from start to finish.
