Our own licensed transfer station

Most contractors hand their waste to someone else. We operate our own licensed waste transfer station, which gives us more control over how materials are handled and processed, and less reliance on third parties.

That means more gets recycled, less goes to landfill, and you get a simpler, more cost-effective service from site to final processing.

What we handle

  • General construction and demolition waste
  • Materials segregated on site to maximise reuse and recycling
  • Concrete crushed and reused on site where suitable
  • Specialist and controlled waste streams, handled appropriately
  • Documentation and traceability for every load

As part of a job, or on its own

Waste management is built into all our demolition, soft strip and dismantling work. We can also provide it as a standalone service for your project.
