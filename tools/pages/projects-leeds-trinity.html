The project

We carried out refurbishment and strip-out works across several retail units in Leeds Trinity Shopping Centre, working for two separate principal contractors at once.

Most of the work was removing existing floor screed so the units could be refurbished, along with smaller internal demolition and refurbishment tasks across individual stores.

Night work in a live shopping centre

To avoid disrupting shoppers and tenants, all the work was done at night. Every work area had to be left safe, clean and secure at the end of each shift, because other contractors, staff and the public would be using those areas the next day.

Running two contractors' programmes across multiple stores at once also took careful planning and coordination to keep everything on schedule.

What we did

  • Strip out of multiple retail units
  • Removed large quantities of floor screed
  • Minor internal demolition and refurbishment works
  • Night-time and out-of-hours working
  • Delivered for two principal contractors at once
  • Prepared and handed over work areas every shift

The outcome

The works were delivered across multiple units without affecting the centre's daytime trading, through night working, good coordination between two contractors and strict end-of-shift procedures.
