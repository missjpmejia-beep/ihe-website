Planned around your site

No two demolition jobs are the same. Before we start, we look at the structure, what's around it, how we'll get in and out, and what needs to happen to the materials. Then we plan the work to keep people safe, keep disruption down and keep your programme on track.

We work directly for owners and developers, and as a subcontractor to principal contractors. Either way, you'll deal with the same family team from first visit to final handover.

What's included

  • Site visit, method statement and risk assessment
  • Controlled demolition of whole buildings or parts of buildings
  • Asbestos identified and removed before demolition starts
  • Concrete breaking, crushing and reuse on site where suitable
  • Waste segregated and processed through our own licensed transfer station
  • Full site clearance, ready for the next stage

Safety comes first

Every job is planned with the safety of our team, your staff, the public and neighbouring properties in mind. We're NFDC members and Constructionline Gold accredited, so the paperwork your procurement team needs is already in place.
