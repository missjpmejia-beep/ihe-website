Services

Soft strip and strip out

Internal fixtures, fittings and finishes removed safely, ready for refurbishment or redevelopment.

Ready for the next trade

Our soft strip team removes ceilings, floor finishes, partitions, fixtures, fittings and redundant services, leaving the space clear and ready for refurbishment, alteration or full demolition.

We work in commercial, retail, education, accommodation and healthcare buildings, including ones that stay open while we work.

Working in live buildings

When a building is still in use, how we work matters as much as what we remove. We plan access, segregation, dust and noise control and waste movements around the people using the building. Where it helps, we work in phases, at night or out of hours.

  • Ceilings, raised floors and floor finishes
  • Partitions and internal walls
  • Fixtures, fittings and redundant services
  • Screed removal
  • Phased, night and out-of-hours working
  • Areas left safe and secure at the end of every shift
