About us

A family business with over 30 years in demolition

Based in South Kirkby, West Yorkshire, we work for councils, contractors, developers, businesses and private clients across the region.

Who we are

IHE Demolition is a family-owned business built on trust and doing the job properly. We've spent more than 30 years in the industry, and we've built our reputation on a strong safety record, reliability and clear communication.

Because we're family-run, you deal directly with the people doing the work. We get to know your project, keep you updated, and take responsibility for getting it right.

What we do

We handle everything from soft strip and dismantling through to full demolition, asbestos removal and site clearance. With our own licensed waste transfer station, we also manage the waste ourselves, from site to final processing.
